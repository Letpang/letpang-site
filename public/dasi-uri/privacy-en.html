Rainbow Bridge: Pet Memorial | Privacy Policy

Letpang Studio operates Rainbow Bridge: Pet Memorial.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your pet’s name and appearance, memorial dates, guardian avatar, letters, activity history, selected photos, and keepsake images on your device. It uses these to display your memorial and memories. No account is required.

Photo processing

If you choose to create a watercolor portrait, the app sends one pet photo, the pet type, and the selected style over HTTPS to Cloudflare Workers AI to create your portrait. Our server does not separately store the original photo or the result. The service uses IP addresses to limit requests. You can choose a built-in illustration instead. The app has no ads or usage analytics. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Retention and deletion

Local records stay on your device until you clear the app’s storage on Android or delete the app on iOS. You can manage separate copies in your device backups or photo library.

Contact

For privacy questions or help accessing, correcting, or deleting your records, contact hello@letpang.com. We use your email address and message to respond and delete them after handling your inquiry unless the law requires us to retain them.
