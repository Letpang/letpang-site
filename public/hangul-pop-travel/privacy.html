Privacy Policy

Letpang Studio · Updated November 7, 2026
Applies to com.letpang.hangulpoptravel on iOS and Android.

Hangul Pop Travel helps you practice travel Korean and create a Korean name card. No account or login is required.

Information stored on your device

Your name, Korean name spelling, introduction, optional Instagram handle, email, phone number, chosen avatar or photo, saved phrases and learning progress are stored locally on your device. We do not upload these details to our servers. When you save or share a card, you choose which image and details to share and the destination. Do not add details you do not want to appear on the card. Removing the app or clearing its app data removes local information; this does not cancel or erase store purchase records.

Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking practice requests microphone and speech recognition permission when you start it. Audio and recognized text are handled by your device's speech recognition service (Apple on iOS or the configured speech provider on Android) and may be processed on the provider's servers according to its settings and policies. We do not store audio or transcripts on our servers. Recognized text is used to compare your attempt with the target sentence, not to identify you or assess native pronunciation. You can decline or revoke permissions in device settings and use other learning features.

Photos and camera

You may choose a photo for your card or use the optional camera/photo reading feature. Selected images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Saving a card may request access to your photo library through your device's save/share interface. These features are optional.

Advertisements — Google AdMob

AdMob may process IP-derived approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver, measure and protect ads. Where required, Google's consent form and Apple's tracking permission let you control relevant uses. Declining tracking does not prevent learning. When required for your region, you can reopen ad privacy choices using the privacy icon in the app navigation. Device settings also provide advertising and permission controls. The optional ad-removal purchase removes banner and full-screen ads.

Google Privacy Policy · AdMob privacy information

Purchases — RevenueCat and the stores

Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment. We do not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase and transaction records, product information and technical data to validate purchases, restore them and provide the ad-free entitlement. Store purchase records can remain after local data is removed. Use Restore purchases to recover an eligible purchase with the same store account.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy

Retention, choices and requests

Local information remains until you change it, clear app data or remove the app. Service providers retain advertising, purchase and security records according to their policies and applicable obligations. We do not sell personal information. This app does not offer account registration, social messaging or public profiles. Contact us for privacy questions or access/deletion requests relating to data available to us. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Children

The app is designed for general travel-language learners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information on our servers. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.
